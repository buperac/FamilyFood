## Rules for retiring

A meal is retired when **both** are true:
1. It was tried twice (the second attempt with adjustments based on first-round feedback)
2. The second attempt still failed — i.e., scored under 3 from a majority of the family

## "Pause" vs. "Retire"

If a meal failed once but with a clear fixable issue (e.g., "too spicy for the 4yo"), it stays in the candidate pool — I'll just adjust the recipe. Only after a second failure does it go here.
