# Best-Value Callouts (Costco vs Walmart)

- **All proteins → Costco.** Per-kg pricing on chicken, pork (and beef when the stash runs out) is meaningfully cheaper than Walmart. The whole-chicken 2-pack and chicken-breast bulk pack are the biggest wins.
- **Rice, oats, frozen berries, cheese blocks → Costco.** Bulk sizes win and they keep.
- **Condiments, specialty (naan, tikka masala, farro, queso fresco), small fresh quantities → Walmart.** Costco would force oversized packs you can't finish.
- **Mozzarella → Costco block, shred yourself.** ~30% cheaper per gram than pre-shredded, melts better.
- **Frozen pizza dough → Walmart Great Value** (~$6 vs ~$10 refrigerated).
- **Produce that spoils → buy small and often (Walmart, Order 2)**, not in Costco volume.
