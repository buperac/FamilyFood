## Tier 2 — Strong Candidates for Upcoming Weeks

### Picadillo (Cuban ground beef)
- Ground beef + tomato + onion + bell pepper + raisins + green olives + cumin + oregano
- Served over rice with optional fried plantains (frozen plantains keep prep fast)
- Sweet + savory + briny — unique flavor profile; raisins are the kid-bridge
- **Cal**: ~580/adult | **Time**: 30 min | **Kid risk**: Medium (depends on raisin tolerance)

### Larb (Thai ground beef lettuce wraps)
- Ground beef cooked with garlic, ginger, fish sauce, lime, shallot, mint, cilantro, toasted rice powder
- Served in butter lettuce cups with sticky rice on the side
- Light and bright vs. heavy comfort food — great summer pick
- **Cal**: ~510/adult | **Time**: 25 min | **Kid risk**: Medium-High (herbs + fish sauce smell)

### Cottage pie
- Ground beef + onion + carrot + peas + Worcestershire + beef broth, topped with mashed potato, baked
- Pure British comfort food, perfect for a cold week
- Massive lunch leftover yield (lunchable next 2 days)
- **Cal**: ~640/adult | **Time**: 60 min | **Kid risk**: Low (mashed potato top is universally loved)

### Greek beef gyro bowls
- Ground beef with oregano + garlic + lemon, served over rice or in pita
- Toppings: tzatziki, cucumber, tomato, red onion, feta
- Mediterranean flavor profile, deconstructable for picky kids
- **Cal**: ~600/adult | **Time**: 30 min | **Kid risk**: Low (build-your-own bowl)

### Italian wedding soup
- Tiny beef meatballs + acini di pepe pasta + escarole or spinach + chicken broth + parmesan
- Cozy, soup-as-dinner option
- Kids love the tiny meatballs (smaller = lower-rejection)
- **Cal**: ~520/adult | **Time**: 35 min | **Kid risk**: Low

### Beef and lentil chili
- Ground beef + black beans + kidney beans + lentils + tomatoes + chili spices
- Stretches ground beef with lentils (so 1 lb of beef serves the family with leftovers)
- Toppings bar: cheese, sour cream, scallions, lime, corn chips
- Freezes well — make double, save half for a future emergency dinner
- **Cal**: ~550/adult | **Time**: 45 min | **Kid risk**: Medium (beans split the kids — some love, some refuse)

### Banh mi-style ground beef sandwiches
- Beef cooked with garlic + soy + fish sauce + sugar
- On a baguette: quick-brined carrot + daikon (10 min in vinegar/sugar/salt), cucumber slices, cilantro, jalapeño (optional), mayo
- Fast and very different from typical ground beef use
- **Cal**: ~640/adult | **Time**: 25 min total | **Kid risk**: Medium (deconstruct for kids)

### Smashburgers
- Ground beef pressed thin on a screaming-hot skillet — Maillard heaven
- American cheese, soft potato bun, dill spears, secret sauce
- Friday treat night option; pair with oven fries
- **Cal**: ~720/adult | **Time**: 25 min | **Kid risk**: Very Low

## Tier 3 — Less Common but Worth Trying

### Beef-stuffed bell peppers
- Filling: beef + rice + tomato + cheese, baked inside halved bell peppers
- For kids who reject "stuffed" food, slice into rings
- **Cal**: ~520/adult | **Time**: 50 min

### Beef and sweet potato hash (breakfast-for-dinner)
- Ground beef + diced sweet potato + onion + spinach + eggs cracked on top
- Single-skillet meal
- **Cal**: ~570/adult | **Time**: 30 min

### Lasagna
- Investment recipe but huge mileage — leftovers for 3 days, freezes well
- **Cal**: ~680/adult | **Time**: 90 min (45 active)

### Beef + zucchini gratin
- Layered ground beef + zucchini + tomato + cream sauce, baked
- Lower-carb option for the adults
- **Cal**: ~510/adult | **Time**: 50 min

### Homemade sloppy joes (not canned)
- Beef + onion + bell pepper + tomato sauce + brown sugar + Worcestershire + mustard
- On potato buns. Kids tend to love this if they've never had the canned version.
- **Cal**: ~580/adult | **Time**: 25 min

### Beef + mushroom stroganoff over egg noodles
- Beef + mushroom + onion + sour cream + Dijon + beef broth, served over wide egg noodles
- Comfort-food classic, very kid-friendly
- **Cal**: ~660/adult | **Time**: 30 min

## Rules for promotion / demotion

- A meal moves from **Tier 2/3 → Tier 1** after it's served and scored 4+/5 from both adults plus at least one of the little kids
- A meal moves to **`retired.md`** after it fails twice
- The ground beef stash isn't infinite — once it's down, we reduce to 1 ground beef meal/week and start buying ground beef like any other protein
