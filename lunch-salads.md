# Interesting Salads (Lunch — for Adult 2 / wife)

Wife wants interesting salads for lunch. "Interesting" = international flavors, grain bowls, protein-loaded options — not just lettuce-and-dressing. Many use dinner leftovers.

Target per salad: 1 serving (just her), 500-650 cal, packs well for next-day if there are leftovers.

This file is the running salad pool. Each week's `meal-plan.md` picks from here based on what's already in the fridge and what dinner leftovers we expect.

> **Allergy note (added 2026-05-25):** Nut allergy in the household — all salads below are nut-free. Watch out for dressings that sneak in peanut/almond (e.g., the old Thai-peanut version is replaced). Granola/seed crackers/protein toppings: check the label.

---

## 1. Mediterranean grain bowl — ~590 cal — pantry/fresh, no leftover dependency
**Ingredients (1 serving)**
- 3/4 cup cooked farro or quinoa (cook a batch on Sunday for the week)
- 1/2 cup chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cucumber, diced
- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 red onion, thin-sliced
- 1/4 cup feta, crumbled
- 8 kalamata olives, halved
- Dressing: 2 tbsp olive oil + 1 tbsp lemon juice + 1 tsp dried oregano + 1 tsp tahini (optional) + salt + pepper

**Method**: combine in a bowl, toss with dressing. Pack the dressing separately if eating later in the day.

---

## 2. Beet + feta + sunflower seed salad with apple — ~540 cal — store-bought beets (nut-free)
**Ingredients (1 serving)**
- 3 cups arugula (or arugula + spinach mix)
- 1 vacuum-packed cooked beet, sliced (Walmart sells these in 250g packs)
- 1/4 cup feta or goat cheese, crumbled
- 2 tbsp toasted sunflower seeds
- 1/2 apple, thin-sliced
- Dressing: 2 tbsp olive oil + 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar + 1 tsp honey + 1 tsp Dijon

**Why it works**: sweet apple + earthy beet + tangy cheese + crunchy seed hits four texture-and-flavor zones at once. No cooking required. (Nut-free swap for the original walnut version.)

---

## 3. Mexican street corn salad — ~480 cal (no protein) / ~620 cal (with protein) — pantry-driven
**Ingredients (1 serving)**
- 1 1/2 cups frozen corn, thawed (or grilled if you have time)
- 1/2 cup black beans, drained
- 1/4 cup cotija or crumbled feta
- 1/4 red onion, diced
- 1/2 jalapeño, seeded and minced (skip if she doesn't want heat)
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- Dressing: juice of 1 lime + 2 tbsp mayo + 1 tsp chili powder + pinch salt
- Optional protein add: 1/2 cup shredded leftover chicken or pulled pork

**Why it works**: corn is sweet, beans are filling, lime + cilantro are bright. Mexican without being a taco.

---

## 4. Mediterranean kofta bowl salad — ~590 cal — uses Wed dinner leftover kofta
**Ingredients (1 serving)**
- 4-5 leftover beef kofta meatballs, warmed
- 2 cups chopped romaine + arugula
- 1/2 cup cooked rice OR 1/2 cup chickpeas (or both for a heartier lunch)
- 1/2 cucumber, diced
- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 red onion, thin-sliced
- 2 tbsp crumbled feta
- Dressing: 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt + 1 tbsp lemon juice + 1 tsp olive oil + 1 tbsp chopped mint or cilantro + salt

**Why it works**: mild kofta + cool yogurt-mint dressing is a fresh Mediterranean take that repurposes Wednesday's dinner cleanly. No fish.

---

## 5. Asian chicken cabbage slaw — ~600 cal — uses leftover roast chicken (nut-free swap for Thai peanut salad)
**Ingredients (1 serving)**
- 2 cups shredded napa cabbage + romaine mix
- 1 cup shredded leftover chicken
- 1/2 cup shredded carrot
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 2 tbsp sliced green onion
- 1/4 cup crispy chow mein noodles (verify nut-free on the label)
- Dressing: 2 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tbsp lime juice + 1 tbsp rice vinegar + 1 tsp grated ginger + 1 tsp honey + 1 tbsp neutral oil + 1 tsp sesame oil

---

## 6. Buffalo chicken salad — ~520 cal — uses leftover chicken
**Ingredients (1 serving)**
- 1 cup shredded leftover chicken, tossed with 2 tbsp Frank's RedHot
- 3 cups chopped romaine
- 1/2 cup chopped celery
- 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese (or feta if blue is too strong)
- 1/4 cup shredded carrot
- 2 tbsp ranch or blue cheese dressing

---

## 7. Korean beef-bowl salad — ~620 cal — uses Sat dinner leftover
**Ingredients (1 serving)**
- 1 cup leftover Korean ground beef
- 2 cups chopped romaine + napa cabbage mix
- 1/2 cup cold cooked rice (or skip if going lower-carb)
- 1/4 cucumber, sliced
- 1/4 cup shredded carrot
- Optional: 1 fried egg
- Dressing: 1 tbsp soy + 1 tsp rice vinegar + 1 tsp sesame oil + 1 tsp honey + sesame seeds

---

## 8. Caprese + chicken — ~570 cal — uses leftover roast chicken
**Ingredients (1 serving)**
- 3 cups arugula
- 1 cup shredded leftover chicken
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 4 oz fresh mozzarella, torn into pieces
- Handful of fresh basil, torn
- Dressing: 2 tbsp olive oil + 1 tbsp balsamic glaze + salt + pepper

**Why it works**: light, fresh, summery. Great for a hot day.
