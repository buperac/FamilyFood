# Costco Same-Day vs Walmart.ca — Per-Unit Price Comparison

**Date**: 2026-05-24 | **Delivery address**: 3748 Kidd Crescent SW
**Method**: per-kg / per-unit (not sticker price — pack sizes differ). Costco = Same-Day (Instacart). Walmart = walmart.ca grocery delivery. Store brands preferred (Kirkland vs Great Value).

## The headline finding

**Costco Same-Day runs through Instacart with a ~25-30% markup over in-warehouse pricing.** That markup erases Costco's traditional bulk advantage on most items. **Walmart.ca wins the majority of categories.** Costco only wins where its absolute bulk price is so low it survives the markup — dark-meat chicken and pork tenderloin.

## Delivery economics (important caveat)

Splitting across both stores isn't free:
- **Costco Same-Day**: needs **$150+** for the $10 monthly credit; below that you may pay a delivery fee. The 4 Costco-winner items total only ~$132 — *under* the threshold.
- **Walmart.ca**: has its own free-delivery minimum (~$35-50), easily cleared by the rest of the order.

So a pure per-item split would shrink the Costco order below $150, costing the $10 credit + a possible fee (~$20 swing). The per-item savings on the 4 Costco winners must beat that ~$20.

**The two items that clearly justify a Costco order**: pork tenderloin (~$26 cheaper for our qty) and whole chicken (~$17 cheaper). Thighs (~$3) and drumsticks (~$4) are marginal.

**Option A — Mostly Walmart + small Costco run**: Costco for pork tenderloin + whole chickens + thighs + drumsticks (~$132, accept no credit or pad to $150); Walmart for everything else. Maximizes per-item savings.

**Option C — All Costco** (the cart already built): simplest, but pays the markup on ~$485 of goods — the most expensive option.

Cost-conscious pick: **Option A** if you don't mind two deliveries; **Option B** if you value simplicity. Either beats Option C.
