- **Wed — Chicken Souvlaki Bowls**: chicken breast (cubed, marinated in lemon + garlic + oregano + olive oil), rice or pita, tzatziki, cucumber, tomato, red onion, feta
- **Thu — BBQ Drumsticks**: chicken drumsticks, BBQ sauce, corn on the cob, coleslaw mix + dressing
- **Fri — Pizza/Flatbread**: pizza dough, sauce, mozzarella, toppings, side salad
- **Sat — Beef Tacos**: ground beef (stash), taco seasoning, tortillas, elevated toppings (quick-brined red onion, queso fresco, cilantro, lime, avocado)
- **Sun — Roast Chicken**: whole chicken, potatoes, carrots/green beans, lemon, garlic, herbs (same proven recipe as Week 1)
