# Breakfast Rotation

Kyle usually makes eggs. Goal: keep eggs as the backbone (~3-4 days/week) and add 2-3 non-egg variations to break monotony. Most weekday options must clock under 10 minutes; Sunday is for batch-prep that pays out across the week.

This file is the running breakfast pool. Each week's `meal-plan.md` will pick from here based on what we have on hand and what the week needs.

> **Allergy note (added 2026-05-25):** Nut allergy in the household — all options below are nut-free. **Buy nut-free granola** (some brands contain almonds/pecans/walnuts). No peanut butter in the toppings bars.

---

## Weekday options (under 10 min active time)

### 1. Classic veggie scramble — ~380 cal/adult — 8 min
- 2 eggs/adult, 1/kid; whisk with splash of milk, salt, pepper
- Soft-scramble in butter
- Mix in last 30 sec: handful baby spinach (wilts in seconds) + diced tomato + sprinkle of cheddar
- Toast on the side
- **Why it's in rotation**: Kyle's default with one upgrade (spinach + tomato). Trivial to add.

### 2. Avocado toast + soft-boiled egg — ~410 cal/adult — 9 min
- Soft-boil eggs 6 min (start water, prep toast and avocado while it boils)
- Smash 1/2 avocado on whole-grain toast with salt + lemon + pinch of chili flakes
- Sliced egg on top
- **Why**: feels like a café breakfast, costs $2.

### 3. Greek yogurt + granola + berries + honey — ~410 cal/adult — 3 min, no cooking
- 1 cup full-fat Greek yogurt
- 1/4 cup granola
- 1/2 cup mixed berries (frozen, microwave 20 sec to thaw and warm)
- Drizzle of honey
- **Why**: highest protein-per-minute breakfast on the list. Kid-loved.

### 4. Smoothie bowl — ~440 cal/adult — 5 min
- Blend: 1 frozen banana + 1 cup frozen berries + 1 scoop protein powder + 3/4 cup milk
- Pour into bowl; top with granola + chia seeds + sliced banana
- **Why**: thick enough to feel like a meal, kids think it's dessert.

### 5. Cottage cheese power bowl — ~330 cal/adult — 3 min
- 1 cup cottage cheese (2%)
- **Savory version**: diced tomato + cucumber + black pepper + drizzle olive oil + everything bagel seasoning
- **Sweet version**: peach (or frozen, thawed) + honey + cinnamon + sunflower seeds (nut-free swap for walnuts)
- **Why**: highest protein-to-cal ratio on the list; great post-workout.

## Sunday-prep options (0 active min on weekdays)

### 6. Overnight oats — ~430 cal/adult — Sunday prep, 0 weekday min
**Per adult per day**:
- 1/2 cup rolled oats + 1/2 cup milk + 2 tbsp Greek yogurt + 1 tbsp chia seeds + 1 tbsp maple syrup + pinch salt
- Stir in a mason jar, refrigerate overnight (or up to 4 days)

**Sunday**: make 4 jars per adult who wants them, eat through Thursday morning.

**Day-of toppings** (pick one): berries, banana + chia seeds, diced apple + cinnamon, chocolate chips + raspberry. (No peanut butter — allergy.)

### 7. Egg muffins / mini frittatas — ~280 cal/adult (2 muffins) — Sunday bake, 30 sec weekday
- 12 eggs + 1/2 cup milk + 1/2 tsp salt + pepper, whisked
- Pour into greased muffin tin (fills 12 cavities)
- Add mix-ins to each cavity: diced ham, spinach, cheese, bell pepper, mushroom (vary!)
- Bake 350°F for 22-25 min until set
- Refrigerate. Microwave 2 muffins for 30 sec each morning.

### 8. Breakfast burritos (freezer batch) — ~520 cal/adult — Sunday prep, 90 sec weekday
- Scramble 12 eggs with diced bell pepper + onion + chorizo or breakfast sausage
- Fill 8 tortillas with eggs + cheese + a spoon of salsa
- Wrap each in foil, freeze
- Weekday: microwave foil-wrapped burrito 90 sec, eat

## Weekend (Saturday or Sunday — slower, family-style)

### 9. Pancakes or waffles — ~500 cal/adult — 30 min
- Double-batch the mix. Freeze half the pancakes/waffles between parchment in a freezer bag.
- Weekday reheats: toaster, 90 sec, done.
- Topping bar: butter, maple syrup, berries, banana slices, whipped cream, lemon curd for special days. (No peanut butter — nut allergy.)

### 10. Breakfast sausage hash — ~620 cal/adult — 35 min
- Dice 4 yellow potatoes; brown in skillet with oil + diced onion + diced bell pepper, 12 min
- Add crumbled breakfast sausage (1 lb), brown 6 min
- Make 5-6 wells in the hash; crack an egg into each. Lid on, cook 4-5 min until eggs set
- Top with hot sauce / salsa / chives
- **Bonus**: this is also a dinner option (see `ground-beef-ideas.md` for a beef hash variant)
