## Monday — Sheet Pan Chicken Fajitas

**Ingredients (5 servings)**
- 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, sliced into thin strips
- 3 bell peppers (mixed red/yellow/green), sliced
- 1 large yellow onion, sliced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 packet fajita seasoning (or DIY: 2 tsp chili powder + 1.5 tsp cumin + 1 tsp paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + 1/2 tsp salt)
- 10 small flour tortillas
- Toppings: shredded cheddar, salsa, sour cream, lime wedges, cilantro

**Method**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. On a large sheet pan, toss chicken, peppers, and onions with olive oil and seasoning.
3. Roast 20-22 min, stirring halfway, until chicken hits 165°F internal and veg edges char.
4. Warm tortillas (10 sec each in microwave, or wrapped in foil in oven last 5 min).
5. Serve buffet-style.

**Calories**: ~580/adult (2 fajitas + moderate toppings)

**Kid hacks**
- Pull 1/4 of the chicken strips off the pan before seasoning for the 4yo if they reject spice
- 6yo + 4yo often eat plain tortilla + cheese + chicken — let them
- Lime wedge is optional, kids skip it

## Tuesday — Spaghetti + Ground Beef Bolognese, Caesar Salad

**Uses your ground beef stash — no need to buy more beef.**

**Ingredients (5 servings)**
- 1 lb spaghetti (whole wheat preferred for fiber, swap if 4yo refuses)
- 1.5 lb ground beef (from freezer — thaw overnight in fridge)
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
- 1 tbsp tomato paste (optional — deepens flavor noticeably)
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- Salt, pepper
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- Caesar: 1 head romaine (or 3 hearts), chopped; 1 cup croutons; 1/3 cup Caesar dressing; shaved parm

**Method**
1. Brown beef in olive oil over medium-high, breaking it up with a wooden spoon, 7-8 min. **Drain excess fat** (key — beef releases more than turkey did).
2. Add onion + garlic to the pan. Cook 4 min until softened. Salt + pepper.
3. Stir in tomato paste, cook 1 min until darkened. Add marinara + Italian seasoning. Simmer **20-25 min** (5 min longer than turkey version — gives beef proteins more time to break down and tenderize).
4. Boil spaghetti to al dente per package.
5. Toss pasta with sauce. Top with parm.
6. Toss Caesar in a separate bowl just before serving.

**Calories**: ~830/adult (beef is fattier than turkey — ~50 cal more per serving)

**Kid hacks**
- Reserve a small portion of plain noodles with butter + parm for the 4yo (zero sauce)
- Beef Bolognese smell often wins kids who reject plain ground beef tacos — same protein, totally different experience

**Cost**: ~$8 (pasta + jar + parm + romaine + croutons + dressing), since beef is from freezer. Saves ~$16 vs the ground turkey version.

**Leftover plan**: 1-1.5 cups of sauce should remain → great over greens for the wife's Wed or Thu lunch, or freeze for a future quick pasta night.

## Wednesday — Beef Kofta in Tikka Masala over Basmati Rice

**Replaced the original salmon (family doesn't like fish). Uses your ground beef stash. Mild, creamy, kid-friendly meatball format — a new flavor lane for the week.**

**Ingredients (5 servings)**
- **Kofta meatballs**: 1.5 lb ground beef (from freezer) + 1/2 cup breadcrumbs + 1 egg + 3 cloves garlic minced + 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger + 1 tsp garam masala + 1 tsp cumin + 1 tsp salt + 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- **Sauce**: 1 jar (~400-650 ml) mild tikka masala simmer sauce (store-bought = fast). *Homemade option*: 1 onion diced + 2 tbsp butter + 1 can (398 ml) crushed tomatoes + 1/2 cup cream + 1 tbsp garam masala + 1 tsp paprika + salt
- 1.5 cups basmati rice (uncooked)
- Optional: 2-4 naan, store-bought, warmed
- Cilantro for garnish

**Method**
1. Cook basmati rice.
2. Mix all kofta ingredients in a bowl. Roll into ~20 small meatballs (small = cooks faster, lower kid-rejection).
3. Brown meatballs in a skillet over medium-high, 6-8 min, turning to brown all sides (or bake at 400°F for 15 min).
4. Pour tikka masala sauce over meatballs. Simmer 10 min until meatballs are cooked through (internal 160°F) and sauce is hot.
5. Serve over basmati rice with warm naan. Garnish with cilantro.

**Calories**: ~620/adult (meatballs + ~1 cup rice + creamy sauce + optional naan adds ~150 if eaten)

**Kid hacks**
- Meatballs + mild creamy sauce is an easy kid sell. For the 4yo: pull 3-4 plain browned meatballs *before* adding sauce; serve with plain rice and sauce on the side for dipping.
- Make sure the jarred sauce is **mild**, not medium/hot — read the label. Adults can add a pinch of cayenne or hot sauce at the table.
- Naan is a strong kid-bridge — even a kid who refuses the meatballs will eat naan + rice.

**Cost**: ~$8-10 (sauce + breadcrumbs + rice + optional naan), since beef is from freezer. Removing the salmon saves ~$24; the masala ingredients add back ~$12. Net ~$12 cheaper than the salmon night.
- Always serve at least one safe carb (rice) per meal — kids who skip the protein will fill on rice and broccoli

## Thursday — Slow Cooker Pulled Pork Tacos

**Ingredients (5 servings + leftovers for Friday/Saturday lunches)**
- 3 lb boneless pork shoulder
- Spice rub: 1 tbsp brown sugar + 1 tsp salt + 1 tsp smoked paprika + 1 tsp cumin + 1 tsp garlic powder + 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1 cup chicken broth (or water)
- 1 yellow onion, sliced
- 10-12 small flour or corn tortillas
- 1 bag coleslaw mix (14 oz)
- 1/3 cup coleslaw dressing
- Toppings: lime wedges, cilantro, shredded cheese
- Optional: 1/2 cup BBQ sauce for those who want it

**Method**
1. **Morning** (8 hrs before dinner): rub pork with spice mix. Place in slow cooker with broth and onion. Low 8 hrs (or High 5 hrs if you start at lunch).
2. **30 min before serving**: mix slaw with dressing (any earlier and it gets watery).
3. Shred pork with two forks directly in the slow cooker. Mix into the juices.
4. Warm tortillas. Serve buffet-style: pork, slaw, cheese, cilantro, lime, optional BBQ.

**Calories**: ~600/adult

**Kid hacks**
- For the 4yo: plain pork + cheese in tortilla, slaw on the side as "crunchy" sticks
- Set aside 2 cups of pork before mixing with BBQ for Friday lunch sandwiches

## Friday — Build-Your-Own Pizza Night

**Ingredients (3 pizzas, ~5 servings)**
- 3 refrigerated pizza doughs (or 2 family-size)
- 1 jar (15 oz) pizza sauce
- 16 oz shredded mozzarella (or shred a block — cheaper and melts better)
- Toppings (your pick): pepperoni, sliced mushrooms, sliced bell pepper, sliced olives, fresh basil, baby spinach
- Side: simple green salad — romaine + cherry tomatoes + vinaigrette

**Method**
1. Preheat oven to 475°F with a pizza stone or upside-down sheet pan inside (gives a crispier bottom).
2. Stretch each dough on parchment paper to ~12".
3. Spread thin sauce layer, then cheese, then toppings. Kids pick their own toppings on their pizza.
4. Slide parchment + pizza onto hot stone/pan. Bake 10-12 min until cheese is bubbly and crust is golden.
5. Toss salad. Slice pizzas.

**Calories**: ~820/adult (3 slices of homemade pizza w/ mozzarella + pepperoni runs ~270 cal each, plus salad with dressing. If you eat 2 slices, you're closer to 580.)

**Kid hacks**
- Pizza Friday is the easiest win in the week — leverage it
- Even the 4yo will participate if they can sprinkle the cheese themselves
- Use leftover bell peppers + mushrooms from earlier in the week — zero waste

**Calorie management**: this is the heaviest meal of the week. If you're calorie-conscious, do 2 slices + double salad instead of 3 slices = ~580 cal/adult.

## Saturday — Korean-Style Ground Beef Bowls

**A "clever ground beef" pick — sweet-savory glaze, kid-friendly bowls, finishes in 25 minutes. Uses your freezer stash, same rice + carrots as elsewhere in the week.**

**Ingredients (5 servings)**
- 1.5 lb ground beef (from freezer)
- 1.5 cups jasmine rice (uncooked)
- 2 carrots, julienned (or bagged matchsticks)
- 1 English cucumber, sliced thin
- 2 cups broccoli florets (leftover from Wed is ideal — even better steamed and tossed with sesame oil)
- 5 eggs (optional but excellent — one fried sunny-side per bowl)
- Sauce: 1/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce + 1/4 cup brown sugar + 2 tbsp sesame oil + 4 cloves garlic minced + 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger + 1 tsp gochujang OR sriracha (start with 1 tsp; adults add more at the table) + 1 tsp black pepper
- 4 green onions, sliced thin
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds (garnish)

**Method**
1. Cook rice (rice cooker or stovetop, ~20 min).
2. Whisk sauce in a small bowl.
3. Heat a large skillet over medium-high. Brown ground beef 6-7 min, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. **Drain excess fat.**
4. Pour sauce over beef. Stir to coat. Cook 2-3 min until glossy and slightly reduced.
5. **Optional veg**: steam broccoli 4 min, then toss with 1 tsp soy + 1 tsp sesame oil. Or skip — raw cucumber + carrot is plenty of veg.
6. **Optional egg**: fry 5 eggs in butter, sunny side up.
7. **Assemble bowls**: rice base, beef on top, raw cucumber + carrot on the side, fried egg on top, garnish with green onions + sesame seeds.

**Calories**: ~590/adult (with egg) / ~520 without

**Kid hacks**
- This is the easiest "build your own" bowl in the week. For the 4yo: small mound of plain rice + small mound of beef (without gochujang in their portion) + cucumber slices as dippers
- **Don't add gochujang to the kid portion of the sauce** — keep it neutral sweet-savory. Adults add sriracha at the table.
- Raw cucumber and carrot are way easier on picky eaters than sauced stir-fry veg

**Cost**: ~$6-8 (rice + carrots + cucumber + soy/sugar/sesame from pantry), since beef is from freezer. Saves ~$22 vs the sirloin stir-fry version.

**Leftover plan**: 1 cup leftover beef + rice → wife's Korean beef bowl salad for Sunday lunch (see `lunch-salads.md`).

## Sunday — Roast Chicken with Potatoes and Green Beans

**Ingredients (5 servings + Monday lunch leftovers)**
- 1 whole chicken (~5 lb) — or 2 small 3-lb birds for more white meat
- 2 tbsp softened butter
- 1 lemon, halved
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- Fresh thyme or rosemary (small bunch) — or 1 tsp dried each
- Salt, pepper, paprika
- 2 lb baby potatoes, halved (or russets cubed)
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb fresh green beans, trimmed
- 2 tbsp butter + squeeze of lemon for green beans

**Method**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Pat chicken thoroughly dry (key for crispy skin).
2. Rub all over with softened butter, salt, pepper, paprika. Stuff cavity with lemon halves, garlic, herbs.
3. Toss potatoes with olive oil + salt + pepper. Spread in a large roasting pan or sheet pan. Set chicken on top of (or alongside) potatoes.
4. Roast 60-75 min. Chicken is done when thigh reads 175°F internal and juices run clear. Potatoes should be golden.
5. Rest chicken 10 min before carving (skip and you lose all the juice).
6. **Last 12 min**: steam green beans 4 min, then sauté in butter with salt + a squeeze of lemon.

**Calories**: ~640/adult

**Kid hacks**
- Drumsticks are kid magnets — give them to the 4yo and 6yo
- Save leftover white meat for Monday lunch: chicken + mayo + celery wraps, or chicken on bread

**Bonus**: save the carcass. Even 30 min in water with onion + carrot scraps = real chicken stock for next week.
