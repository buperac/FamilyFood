## "Best value" callouts

- **Shred your own mozzarella** — Costco block mozzarella is ~30% cheaper per gram than pre-shredded, and melts noticeably better on pizza.
- **Don't buy Costco onions** (10 lb bag) unless you cook daily — half will sprout before you use them. Use Walmart 3 lb bag.
- **Salmon at Costco** = the best price on quality salmon in Canada by a meaningful margin. Frozen is fine if fresh is twice the price.
- **Whole rotisserie chicken at Costco ($7.99)** is a backup option for Sunday if you don't want to roast — but you lose the leftover potatoes/pan drippings, so I'd still recommend roasting your own at least every other week.
- **Skip Walmart for proteins** — almost always cheaper per kg at Costco.
