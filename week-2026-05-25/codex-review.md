- Pizza dough at Walmart confirmed correct.
- **Mozzarella price error**: Costco Kirkland 1.15kg is ~$18.79, not the $12 I assumed.
- Frozen Costco salmon portions give better portion control + lower per-serving cost than fresh fillet for weeknight use.
