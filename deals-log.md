> **The live canonical version is the "Deals" tab** of the *Gronning Family Kitchen — Tracker* Google Sheet in the **gronningk@gmail.com** account (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1N_dke4EfsLF8WXnkuRNRFUwF6AwCErqXEXS4M5YFGyM/edit). Read/write it via Claude-in-Chrome (the Drive connector is the work account and can't see it). This markdown file is the offline fallback.

Standout grocery deals worth buying extra of, spotted during ordering sessions. Logged by the **grocery-shopping** skill when an item is notably below its baseline price (baseline lives in the **Ingredient Prices** sheet).

**Stock-up rule:** only stock up on **shelf-stable or freezable** items the family actually uses (rice, oil, canned goods, proteins to portion + freeze, frozen veg/berries). **Never** stock up on perishables (leafy greens, fresh herbs, dairy, ripe fruit) — the spoilage wipes out the saving.

**How a deal qualifies:** a sale badge, or a per-unit price clearly below the recorded baseline (rule of thumb: ~15%+ under). Always record the **per-unit** price ($/kg, $/100 g, $/L, $/count), not the sticker.
